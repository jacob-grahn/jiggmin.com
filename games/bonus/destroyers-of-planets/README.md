# Destroyers of Planets (preview)

Recovered from the author's archived demo page:
https://web.archive.org/web/20070302105618/http://jiggmin.com/DoP.html

Original asset: `DoP29.swf`, fetched via
https://web.archive.org/web/20070302105618id_/http://jiggmin.com/DoP29.swf

`game.swf` is unmodified. Its byte length, SHA-256 and SWF header metadata are in
`data/bonus-games.json`. `title-screen.jpg` is a browser screenshot of the original
550 × 350 title screen rendered with the project's vendored Ruffle, not recreated art.
Eight frames were captured with a one-second delay between captures. The label uses
frame 8 with lighten overlays from frames 1 and 6 over the top word (x=35, y=20,
475 × 105 pixels), retaining the brighter original highlights in “Destroyers”.
The rest of the image uses frame 8. No lettering was redrawn.
Use `/tests/fixtures/destroyers-of-planets.html` on the source preview to inspect it.

Arrow keys move; A runs/interacts; S changes weapons; D changes characters.
The virtual controller maps the joystick to arrow keys and its buttons to A and D.
The joystick direction layout is rotated 45 degrees clockwise: top right sends Up,
bottom right sends Right, bottom left sends Down, and top left sends Left.
Weapon switching (S) remains available on a keyboard.
Title, starting room, weapon switching and character switching were checked.
The demo is unfinished; full completion has not been verified.

Earn the cartridge by winning every cup round through ten cups. Collection is saved
through the normal bonus-cartridge store and delivered on the next den visit.
